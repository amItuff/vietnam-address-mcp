## Provenance of the underlying data

Both upstream projects state that they are not affiliated with the Vietnamese government and
derive their data from public administrative sources:

- Names and codes: the General Statistics Office / National Statistics Office administrative
  units catalogue (`danhmuchanhchinh.nso.gov.vn`) and Decision 19/2025/QĐ-TTg.
- Old → new mapping: assembled by the upstream author from the Resolutions of the National
  Assembly Standing Committee on the 2025 commune-level reorganisation.
- For old wards that were divided between several new wards, the upstream author chose a
  default target by comparing the old ward's geocoded centre point with the new wards'
  boundaries (boundaries from `sapnhap.bando.com.vn`, centre points from a geocoding API).
  Only the resulting choice is included here; no coordinates or boundaries are redistributed.

This project makes no claim that the upstream sources are authoritative. For legal or
contractual use, confirm against the official resolutions.
